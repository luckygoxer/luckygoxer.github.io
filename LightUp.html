About:

This is an IMT&S Project for the Hengelo Light Festival for Hengelo Promotie, where roughly 10.000 to 15.000 people are expected to come on both nights and have the opportunity to play our game. Our game will be projected on the wall of the SPACE building in Hengelo! People will be able to participate by scanning a QR code with their phone and connecting to the server. Build your own spaceship, zap other players down, and make sure to last as long as possible! Feel free to join us for free on February 12th and 13th, 2027, to check out the game we made!
