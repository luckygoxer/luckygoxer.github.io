Critter Town

January 31, 2025

About:

Critter Town is a dungeon crawler mixed with a town builder, made for Project Startup. For this unique game, I have helped develop the game concept and mechanics. I also did the marketing and advertising. I was in charge of the social media accounts and created the accounts for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and X and all the content on them.

This game was created in three weeks.
